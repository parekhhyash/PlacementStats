About · Method & analytics · SPIT 2027

How this is made.

01Why this exists

We built this portal to make placement statistics transparent, structured and accessible for students of Sardar Patel Institute of Technology (SPIT).

Placement notices are often scattered across circulars and groups. This dashboard brings all 45 recruiting companies together into a single, cohesive interface: roles offered, compensation figures, stipends, and confirmed selections across our branches.

It is made for everyone in SPIT's Class of 2027 and juniors preparing for upcoming campus placement seasons.

02What this is

A comprehensive, verified record of campus recruitment for Sardar Patel Institute of Technology (SPIT) Mumbai, covering Computer Engineering (CSE), Information Technology (IT), Electronics & Telecommunication (EXTC), and Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS).

All data has been compiled directly from placement cell reports, company offer letters, and student selection records.

03Where the numbers come from

Every statistic, package, and shortlist is drawn directly from campus recruitment drives, company offer letters, and student-reported placement reports.

Waitlisted candidates and non-employment competitions (such as hackathon finalists) are explicitly noted and categorized to ensure statistical integrity.

04What the numbers mean

CTC (Cost to Company): Reflects total annualized advertised compensation, including fixed base pay, performance bonuses, joining incentives, and retention bonuses where reported.

Stipend: Monthly compensation during internship period (ranging up to ₹1,60,000/month at JPMC).

Median vs Average: Median CTC (₹13.0 LPA) reflects the middle benchmark for placed students, while the average CTC (₹13.86 LPA) reflects total batch compensation scale.

05Privacy & Policy

We respect individual privacy. No personal contact details, roll numbers, or sensitive student records are published on this dashboard. All aggregated figures serve informational and community analytics purposes.

If you identify any discrepancy or have an updated figure, please use the correction report form.
